Questions

Frequently asked questions

I'm not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what we're here for. You tell us what you want, and we handle the design and the details. If you can send a text, you can work with us.

Do you run the ads for me too?

We design the ads and give you files sized for each platform. If you want, we can walk you through boosting a post or setting up a simple ad on Facebook or Instagram.

What do I need to get started?

Your logo if you have one, a few photos of your products or food, and what you want to promote. If you don't have good photos, we can figure it out together.

How long does it take?

About one to two weeks after your first deposit, depending on the work.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Every project includes a round of changes. Just text us what you want different and we'll take care of it.

Can my marketing be in Spanish?

Yes. We can make your ads, posts, and menus in English, Spanish, or both.

Do you only work with businesses in one area?

No. We work with businesses anywhere. We do everything by phone, text, and email, so it doesn't matter where you are.
