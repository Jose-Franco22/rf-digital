Questions

Frequently asked questions

I'm not good with computers. Is that a problem?

Not at all. That's what I'm here for. You tell me what you want, and I handle the setup, the design, and the tech. If you can send a text, you can work with me.

What does the $100 setup cover?

It covers designing and building your site, getting your photos and info in place, and connecting your web address. After that, you just pay the monthly plan.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, hours, phone number, and a few photos. If you have a menu or a list of services and prices, bring that too. If you don't have photos, we can figure it out together.

How long does it take?

Most basic sites are ready in about a week once I have your info. Booking and online ordering take a little longer to set up and test.

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Text me what you want changed, like new hours, a new service, or a price update, and I'll take care of it.

Can my site be in Spanish?

Yes. I can build your site in English, Spanish, or both with a language switch at the top, just like this one.

Do you only work with businesses in Roma?

I'm based in Roma and love working with local businesses I can meet in person, but I work with businesses anywhere. We can do everything by phone and text.
